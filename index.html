Who We Are

Built on the Belief That Poverty Should Never Decide Who Gets an Education

Life Changers Education, Research and Medical Foundation (LCERMF) is a California-based 501(c)(3) nonprofit dedicated to two things: keeping qualified Nigerian students in school, and protecting their communities from malaria.

We are a lean, transparent, governance-first organization. Our founders believe that a dollar donated should do exactly what it promises. That is why we have written policies, independent oversight, and zero tolerance for administrative waste.

"We see a future where every Nigerian student has equal access to education, and where malaria is no longer a barrier to health, economic stability, and national development."

Our Programs

Two Programs. One Purpose.

Everything we do is built around a single outcomegiving Nigerian children and communities a real shot at a better life.

Education

Education Access & Scholarship Program

Qualified students are dropping outnot because they failed, but because they ran out of money. We fix that.

  • What it does: Provides tuition, books, and academic fees directly to the institution
  • Who it helps: Low-income university students across Nigeria with proven academic potential
  • What changes: Students stay enrolled, graduate, and give back to their communities

  • Ongoing mentorship and academic monitoring from enrollment to graduation

Malaria Prevention & Community Health Initiative

A Child Dies Every 2 Minutes

While you read this page, a child somewhere in sub-Saharan Africa will die from malaria. Not from lack of medicine. From lack of accessto nets, education, and community health workers who know how to prevent it.

Nigeria Carries 27% of the Global Burden

Nigeria alone accounts for more than a quarter of all malaria cases and deaths worldwide. The communities LCERMF serves are at the centre of this crisis. Grassroots preventionnets, education, awarenessis the most effective tool available.

Source: WHO & Africa CDC 2023
💸

$12 Billion Lost to Malaria Every Year

Beyond the human cost, malaria drains $12 billion annually from African economies through lost productivity, healthcare costs, and generational poverty. Preventing one case does not just save a lifeit protects a family's economic future.

Source: African Leaders Malaria Alliance (ALMA)

A $2 Net Can Save a Child's Life

Insecticide-treated mosquito nets are one of the most cost-effective public health interventions ever documented. One net, properly used, can protect a family for years. Yet millions of children in rural Nigeria sleep without one tonight.

Source: UNICEF & Roll Back Malaria Partnership

Progress Is Stalling

After two decades of steady reduction, malaria deaths rose sharply during the COVID-19 pandemic and have not fully recovered. Funding gaps, supply chain disruptions, and community health worker shortages are leaving the most vulnerable behind.

Source: WHO World Malaria Report 2023

Community Education Works

Studies consistently show that grassroots health education reduces malaria transmission by 40–60% in targeted communities. Teaching families how to prevent infectionnot just treat itis the sustainable path to elimination.

Why This Matters

The Gap Between Potential and Opportunity

The Problem

In Nigeria, nearly 10 million young people who qualify for university education never enrollor drop out before finishing. The barrier is almost never ability. It is almost always money. A student who cannot pay term fees is sent home. A family that cannot buy nets watches children get sick from a preventable disease.

What We Do

LCERMF steps in at exactly that point. We identify students who have earned their place in university but cannot fund it. We provide the scholarship. We stay involved. And in the same communities, we teach families how to protect themselves from malariaand give them the nets to do it.

The Result

A student who graduates changes more than her own life. She changes her family's trajectory. She becomes a teacher, a nurse, an engineersomeone who gives back. That is the multiplier effect of education. And it starts with one scholarship funded by one donor like you.

Questions

Everything You Want to Know

We believe in radical transparency. If your question is not here, email us directly.

LCERMF serves low-income and underserved communities in Nigeria. Our scholarship program targets college and university students from financially disadvantaged families who have academic potential but cannot afford to continue their education. Our malaria prevention program reaches grassroots communities with limited access to healthcare and health education.

Yes. LCERMF is a recognized 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization under the U.S. Internal Revenue Code. All donations are fully tax-deductible to the extent permitted by law. You will receive a donation receipt for your records upon giving.

100% of donated funds go directly to programsscholarships paid to educational institutions, malaria prevention materials, and community health workshops. Our founders are not drawing salaries from donated funds at this stage of the organization. We maintain written financial controls and document every expenditure.

Scholarship recipients are selected by an independent Scholarship Committee using written, objective scoring criteria including financial need, academic achievement, and community commitment. No scholarships are awarded to board members, officers, or their family members. Wherever possible, funds are paid directly to the educational institutionnot to the student.

Our Malaria Prevention Initiative runs grassroots education workshops, distributes insecticide-treated mosquito nets, and partners with local health clinics and community leaders. We conduct community surveys to track awareness and prevention outcomes. All Nigerian field operations are managed under written agreements with full documentation and LCERMF retains oversight of all funds.

We are targeting our pilot scholarship cohort launch for 2026. The number of students we can support depends directly on how many founding donors join us now. Every donation at this stage has a direct, traceable impact on how many students we can help in our first year.
